# Hi, I'm building Glumoo 👋

**Science that cares for every pet.**

[Glumoo](https://glumoo.com/) is a pet nutrition brand for cats and dogs. We are building it AI-native: agents help with repeatable research, content, operations, and checks; people remain accountable for product decisions, evidence boundaries, and what gets published.

## Open source for healthier pets

### [Glumoo Pet Health Skill](https://github.com/growing2025666-art/glumoo-pet-health-skill)

A bilingual, safety-first Agent Skill for pet health and nutrition conversations.

- emergency and possible poisoning triage comes first;
- evidence strength and uncertainty are explicit;
- diagnosis and treatment stay with veterinary professionals;
- brand facts are separated from independent health evidence;
- the community can inspect, test, and improve the workflow.

## What we're building

- evidence-aware pet nutrition products and education;
- open AI workflows that turn sources into reviewable work;
- practical tools that help more pet families care for their furry companions safely.
